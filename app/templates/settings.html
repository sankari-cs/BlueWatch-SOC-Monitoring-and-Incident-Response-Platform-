Local security

Demo assumptions

  • Only loopback client addresses are authorized by the application.
  • POST forms require a session CSRF token.
  • Session cookies are HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax.
  • Uploads are size-limited and parsed in memory; uploaded content is not executed.
  • Use a strong BLUEWATCH_SECRET_KEY if session continuity is needed.
  • This application has no analyst login or multi-user role system. It is not production reviewed.
